Two US citizens were executed by federal agents in Minneapolis this month. Neither was breaking any laws. Both were 37 years old. The administration labeled both as violent terrorists. Video evidence contradicts every official claim.

This is state-sponsored terrorism, and the people responsible have names.


## Renee Good

On January 7, 2026, [Renee Good](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/renee-nicole-good-minneapolis-ice-shooting-victim-caring-neighbor-rcna252901) was shot in the head by an ICE agent after dropping her six-year-old son off at school. She was a mother of three from Colorado Springs. She was not a target of any immigration enforcement.

DHS spokesperson Tricia McLaughlin [labeled Good](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/07/ice-dhs-minneapolis-shooting.html) a "violent rioter" who committed "an act of domestic terrorism." President Trump [posted](https://www.cpr.org/2026/01/07/fatal-minneapolis-ice-shooting-colorado-woman/) that she "violently, willfully, and viciously ran over the ICE Officer." Secretary Kristi Noem [called it](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/what-we-know-so-far-about-the-ice-shooting-in-minneapolis) "justified self-defense."

Minneapolis Police Chief Brian O'Hara, who reviewed the video evidence, [said](https://www.washingtonpost.com/nation/2026/01/07/minneapolis-shooting-ice/) it doesn't support self-defense claims. Mayor Jacob Frey [called](https://www.axios.com/local/twin-cities/2026/01/07/ice-shooting-victim-identified-renee-nicole-good-minneapolis) the federal characterization "bullshit" and added: "This was an agent recklessly using power that resulted in somebody dying."


## Alex Pretti

Seventeen days later, on January 24, 2026, [Alex Pretti](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/alex-pretti-fatally-shot-federal-officers-minneapolis-identified-paren-rcna255758) was shot multiple times by Border Patrol agents. He was an ICU nurse at the Minneapolis VA, spending his life caring for veterans. He had no criminal record. He was a lawful gun owner with a valid permit.

Video shows what happened. Federal agents [pushed a woman to the ground](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/alex-pretti-fatally-shot-federal-officers-minneapolis-identified-paren-rcna255758). Pretti stepped between the agent and the woman, putting his arm around her to help her up. He had a phone in one hand. His other hand was raised. He was pepper-sprayed, wrestled to the ground by half a dozen agents, and [shot at least ten times](https://www.washingtonpost.com/investigations/2026/01/25/minneapolis-shooting-video-gun/) in five seconds.

An eyewitness [said in a court document](https://www.npr.org/2026/01/25/nx-s1-5687875/minneapolis-shooting-minnesota-ice-alex-pretti-dhs-investigation) that Pretti was not even facing agents when they grabbed him: "It didn't look like he was trying to resist, just trying to help [a] woman up."

A pediatrician who witnessed the shooting from their apartment [counted](https://kstp.com/kstp-news/top-news/court-filings-witnesses-say-they-didnt-see-pretti-with-a-gun-give-more-details-of-deadly-ice-shooting/) at least four gunshot wounds on Pretti's body - three to his back, one to his upper left chest.

Video verified by [Reuters and the Wall Street Journal](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/videos-minneapolis-shooting-witnesses/) appears to show an agent removing Pretti's gun and moving away less than a second before another agent fired.

His final act was trying to protect a stranger.

He put his arm around her. He had his phone in one hand, his other hand raised. Watching the video, it sounds to me like his last words were "are you ok?" - spoken to the woman he was trying to protect.

Then he was pepper-sprayed, tackled by half a dozen agents, and shot ten times in five seconds. Three bullets in his back.

These weren't terrorists. These weren't extremists. These were neighbors. Community members. A mom. A nurse. People who saw something wrong happening and tried to do something about it.

The administration wants you to believe these were dangerous criminals who got what they deserved. The videos show something very different.

"The Party told you to reject the evidence of your eyes and ears. It was their final, most essential command."


## The lies

Within hours of Pretti's death, the administration had their story ready.

**Stephen Miller**, Trump's deputy chief of staff, [called Pretti](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/stephen-miller-accuses-democrats-siding-terrorists-after-minneapolis-border-patrol-involved-shooting) "a would-be assassin."

**Gregory Bovino**, the Border Patrol commander running the Minneapolis operation, [claimed](https://www.notus.org/trump-white-house/minneapolis-shooting-gun-rights) Pretti wanted to do "maximum damage and massacre law enforcement." When asked about Pretti's death, Bovino [told CNN](https://www.npr.org/2026/01/25/nx-s1-5687875/minneapolis-shooting-minnesota-ice-alex-pretti-dhs-investigation): "The victims are the Border Patrol agents. The suspect put himself in that situation."

**Kristi Noem**, Secretary of Homeland Security, [accused Pretti](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/1/25/who-was-alex-pretti-the-nurse-shot-dead-by-federal-agents-in-minneapolis) of being "a domestic terrorist" and said: "He wasn't there to peacefully protest. He was there to perpetuate violence."

**Kash Patel**, FBI Director, [said on Fox News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/trump-administration/trump-administration-second-amendment-rights-minneapolis-shooting-rcna255784): "You cannot bring a firearm loaded with multiple magazines to any sort of protest that you want. It's that simple."

Representative Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez [pointed out](https://web.archive.org/web/20260125081957/https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/live/2026/jan/25/minneapolis-shooting-parents-of-alex-pretti-say-trump-officials-are-telling-sickening-lies) the contradiction: "How rich is it that [Kristi Noem] is saying showing up to the scene of a protest with a legally owned weapon should be grounds for a person's death, execution at the hands of the state, by the same party and the same administration that praises Kyle Rittenhouse."

The National Rifle Association and Gun Owners of America - not organizations known for criticizing Republican administrations - [both criticized](https://thereload.com/several-gun-rights-groups-call-for-investigation-into-ice-killing-of-ccw-permittee/) the official statements.

Pretti's parents [responded](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/man-shot-and-killed-by-federal-officers-in-minnesota-was-an-icu-nurse-his-parents-say): "Alex is clearly not holding a gun when attacked by Trump's murdering and cowardly ICE thugs. He has his phone in his right hand and his empty left hand is raised above his head while trying to protect the woman ICE just pushed down all while being pepper sprayed."

They added: "The sickening lies told about our son by the administration are reprehensible and disgusting."


## The federal investigation into federal killings

Both times, the federal government moved to control the investigation into its own agents' actions.

After the Good shooting, the FBI [revoked Minnesota investigators' access](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/what-we-know-so-far-about-the-ice-shooting-in-minneapolis) to evidence, reversing an earlier agreement for a joint investigation.

After the Pretti shooting, federal agents [blocked Minneapolis police](https://www.cbsnews.com/minnesota/live-updates/reported-shooting-south-minneapolis-federal-agents-protesters/) and the Minnesota Bureau of Criminal Apprehension from the scene - even after state investigators obtained a judge's signed search warrant. A federal judge had to [grant a restraining order](https://www.cbsnews.com/minnesota/live-updates/reported-shooting-south-minneapolis-federal-agents-protesters/) against DHS to prevent them from destroying evidence.

As of January 25, all agents involved in the Pretti shooting [remain on duty](https://www.notus.org/immigration/bovino-backtracks-after-saying-man-killed-by-cbp-wanted-to-massacre-officers).

Minneapolis Police Chief O'Hara [noted](https://www.cbsnews.com/minnesota/live-updates/reported-shooting-south-minneapolis-federal-agents-protesters/) that of the three homicides in Minneapolis so far in 2026, two were committed by federal immigration agents. "The Minneapolis Police Department went the entire year last year recovering about 900 guns from the street, arresting hundreds and hundreds of violent offenders, and we didn't shoot anyone," he said.

Governor Tim Walz [called it](https://www.npr.org/2026/01/16/nx-s1-5675909/trump-post-online-minnesota-social-media-ice) "the consequences of governance designed to generate fear, headlines, and conflict. It's governing by reality TV, and today that recklessness cost someone their life."


## The shakedown

Hours after Pretti was killed, Attorney General Pam Bondi sent a [three-page letter](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/bondi-minnesota-voter-rolls-welfare-data-walz-immigration-shootings/) to Governor Walz. In it, she demanded Minnesota:

1. Turn over state voter rolls to the Department of Justice
2. Share Medicaid and food stamp (SNAP) recipient data with the federal government
3. Repeal all sanctuary policies
4. Give ICE full access to all state detention facilities

Bondi wrote that these "simple steps will help bring back law and order to Minnesota."

This has nothing to do with immigration. The DOJ has been [demanding Minnesota's voter data for months](https://www.spokesman.com/stories/2026/jan/25/department-of-justice-renews-push-for-voter-welfar/), long before Operation Metro Surge began. Minnesota has refused, saying they fear the data would be used to target individuals. The state is already in [active litigation](https://www.kttc.com/2026/01/25/minnesota-secretary-state-steve-simon-releases-statement-response-us-ag-pam-bondi-letter/) with the DOJ over this demand.

Vice President Vance [confirmed](https://www.spokesman.com/stories/2026/jan/25/department-of-justice-renews-push-for-voter-welfar/) in Minneapolis on January 22 that the administration wants to use Medicaid and SNAP data for immigration enforcement: "What we'd like to do is talk to local officials and say, 'You know what, according to the Medicaid rolls, where was the last address this person was domiciled?'"

Minnesota Secretary of State Steve Simon [responded](https://www.kttc.com/2026/01/25/minnesota-secretary-state-steve-simon-releases-statement-response-us-ag-pam-bondi-letter/) to Bondi's letter: "The answer is no. Her letter is an outrageous attempt to coerce Minnesota into giving the federal government private data on millions of U.S. citizens in violation of state and federal law."

Simon added: "It is deeply disturbing that the U.S. Attorney General would make this unlawful request a part of an apparent ransom to pay for our state's peace and security."

Senator Chris Van Hollen [called it](https://www.notus.org/trump-white-house/pam-bondi-voter-rolls-election-snap-data-trump-administration-demand-letter-tim-walz-minnesota-minneapolis) "blackmail" and said: "What they're saying is: 'We'll stop these lawless activities, we'll stop these killings of American citizens if you hand over your voter registration data.'"

Representative Ruben Gallego [called it](https://www.notus.org/trump-white-house/pam-bondi-voter-rolls-election-snap-data-trump-administration-demand-letter-tim-walz-minnesota-minneapolis) "a shakedown": "They're using fear to get their hands on voter information. In America, you don't get to strong-arm your way into our elections."

DNC Chair Ken Martin [said](https://thehill.com/homenews/state-watch/5705179-bondi-walz-letter-immigration-fraud-voter-rolls/): "They are trying to trade the presence of ICE and the murder and mayhem that they are causing for control of Minnesota's elections. That's just wild."

This is what the killings are for. Not immigration enforcement. Election control.


## The domestic terrorist database

On January 23, 2026 - one day before Pretti was killed - an ICE agent in Maine was [filmed](https://reason.com/2026/01/23/ice-tells-legal-observer-we-have-a-nice-little-database-and-now-youre-considered-a-domestic-terrorist/) photographing a woman's car as she recorded their activity. When she asked why, the agent replied:

"Because we have a nice little database, and now you're considered a domestic terrorist. So have fun with that."

DHS spokesperson Tricia McLaughlin [told Reason](https://reason.com/2026/01/23/ice-tells-legal-observer-we-have-a-nice-little-database-and-now-youre-considered-a-domestic-terrorist/) there is "NO database of 'domestic terrorists' run by DHS." But a [leaked FBI-DHS memo](https://newrepublic.com/post/205629/ice-agent-threat-domestic-terrorism-database-filming-public) obtained by journalist Ken Klippenstein shows the department broadening the definition of domestic violent extremists to include protesters taking "advantage of First Amendment-protected activity" - which includes recording law enforcement.

Court documents [reportedly show](https://www.brennancenter.org/our-work/research-reports/ice-wants-go-after-dissenters-well-immigrants) that Border Patrol commander Gregory Bovino believes all protesters are "violent rioters" and has instructed officers to arrest protesters "who make hyperbolic comments."

Secretary Noem has [said](https://www.brennancenter.org/our-work/research-reports/ice-wants-go-after-dissenters-well-immigrants) that revealing the identities of agents and videotaping them on operations constitutes "violence."

Acting ICE Director Todd Lyons [announced](https://www.brennancenter.org/our-work/research-reports/ice-wants-go-after-dissenters-well-immigrants) that ICE intends to probe anti-ICE protesters and those who support them: "We are going to track the money. We are going to track these ringleaders."

In Minneapolis, Brandon Sigüenza - a volunteer with a local group that monitors ICE activity - [described](https://reason.com/2026/01/23/ice-tells-legal-observer-we-have-a-nice-little-database-and-now-youre-considered-a-domestic-terrorist/) in a sworn court declaration how federal agents surrounded his car, smashed out his windows, roughly arrested him, and detained him for hours. A federal judge [ruled](https://reason.com/2026/01/23/ice-tells-legal-observer-we-have-a-nice-little-database-and-now-youre-considered-a-domestic-terrorist/) that plaintiffs were likely to succeed on their claims that federal officers engaged in targeted First Amendment retaliation. The judge found that the protesters' and monitors' actions "did not forcibly obstruct or impede the agents' work."

You can now be labeled a domestic terrorist for filming police in America.
